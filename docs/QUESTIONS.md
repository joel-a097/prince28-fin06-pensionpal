
# PensionPal - Questions

## Questions to Clarify

1. Which EPFO documents are needed to help employees transfer their PF?
2. What information is required to check pension eligibility?
3. How can an employee check their EPF service history?
4. Which official EPFO sources should be used for the information?
5. Which form is required to transfer PF from a previous employer?
6. What is the exact current PF balance in a specific employee's account without accessing their private EPFO passbook? (Unanswerable)
7. What are the eligibility conditions for EPS pension?
8. Which documents are required when submitting a PF withdrawal claim?
9. How do changes in EPFO circulars affect existing claim procedures?
10. How can an employee raise a grievance if their PF transfer is delayed?

## Notes

- Use official EPFO information.
- Do not guess pension eligibility rules.
- Keep the information simple and understandable.
- Clearly identify questions that cannot be answered using available public sources.
